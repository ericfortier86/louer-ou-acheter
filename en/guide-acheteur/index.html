Your first home.
Your starting checklist.

A practical guide to buying in Gatineau, Hull, Aylmer, Cantley, Val-des-Monts, Buckingham, Chelsea or Masson-Angers. Move forward at your own pace, one step at a time.

01 / Review your budget

  • Work out your available net income and monthly household expenses.
  • List your debts and required payments.
  • Choose a comfortable monthly payment rather than relying only on a lender’s maximum.
  • Keep an emergency fund separate from your buying budget.

02 / Prepare your financing

  • Gather the income and employment documents requested by your mortgage professional.
  • Prepare statements showing the source of your down payment.
  • Review your Equifax and TransUnion reports and report any errors.
  • Ask your mortgage professional which pre-approval options suit your situation.
  • Confirm eligibility for the FHSA and HBP with a professional before using them.

03 / Plan for initial costs

  • Down payment and, if applicable, tax on the mortgage default insurance premium.
  • Inspection, notary and property transfer tax.
  • Tax adjustments, condo fees or other costs at closing.
  • Moving, utility connections, insurance and initial repairs.

04 / Clarify your criteria

  • Areas, commute times and essential services.
  • Property type, bedrooms, parking and future needs.
  • Three must-haves and three preferences you can be flexible about.
  • A realistic timeline and, if applicable, a lease end date to confirm.

05 / Visit and verify

  • Compare properties using the same total budget, including fees and maintenance.
  • Read the seller’s documents and ask questions.
  • For a condo: review the documents, fees and expected work with the appropriate professionals.
  • Discuss financing and inspection conditions and deadlines with your broker before submitting a promise to purchase.

06 / Prepare to take possession

  • Meet the agreed conditions and deadlines.
  • Confirm financing, insurance and the required funds with the professionals involved.
  • Arrange the notary appointment and your move.
  • Plan address changes and utility transfers.

Buying later on? Set a monthly savings goal, a date to review your budget and an initial conversation with a mortgage professional. You do not need to be ready to buy to prepare for what comes next.
